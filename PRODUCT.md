# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and operators of growing businesses who are evaluating flexible outsourced support and deciding whether to book a consultation.

## Product Purpose

Explain OutsourcePro's capabilities clearly, build trust, and turn qualified visitors into consultation enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, and approachable. The experience should feel calm, dependable, and easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Generic AI or SaaS-template decoration, dense enterprise dashboards, and ornamental controls that look interactive but are not.

## Design Principles

- Make services and benefits easy to scan.
- Build confidence through clarity and consistency.
- Use brand marks sparingly and render them consistently.
- Keep calls to action obvious and distinct from decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, descriptive image text, and reduced-motion support.
