# OutsourcePro Full Website

A responsive multi-page static website matching the approved clean black-and-white outsourcing design with pastel accents.

## Included
- Homepage with corrected hero and corrected 3-step process section
- Services overview and 8 individual service pages
- About page
- Contact page
- Pricing page
- Blog index and 6 full article pages
- FAQ, privacy, terms, modal enquiry form, responsive navigation, animations
- Optimized website images and approved design references

## Run locally
Open `index.html` directly, or run a local server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

## Before publishing
1. Replace `OutsourcePro` and the dot logo with your final business name and logo.
2. Replace the example email and social links.
3. For PHP hosting, replace `your-email@example.com` in `send-contact.php`. On a local/static preview, validated submissions are saved in the browser. You may also replace the handler with your CRM, Formspree, Netlify Forms, or another backend.
4. Review the privacy policy and terms with legal counsel.
5. Replace any placeholder claims or pricing language with verified business information.
